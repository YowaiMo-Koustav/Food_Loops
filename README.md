# **FoodLoops: Combatting Food Waste, One Bite at a Time**  
*Turning near-expiry goods into budget-friendly choices 🌍*

[![GitHub license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

---

## 🚀 Overview

**FoodLoops** is a purpose-driven Android application built to address one of the most pressing global challenges—**food waste**. By bridging the gap between consumers and retailers offering discounts on near-expiry products, FoodLoops enables sustainable shopping habits while helping users save money.

> You might think we’ve lost our minds coding this—until the data speaks. Surveys and real-world testing show: supermarkets reduce loss margins, and users increase their savings. A win-win, every time.

This initiative aligns with the **United Nations Sustainable Development Goals**, especially focusing on **Zero Hunger** and **No Poverty**.

Imagine a world where perfectly good food doesn’t end up in landfills—and every purchase contributes to a healthier planet. That’s the vision behind FoodLoops.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🛍️ Dynamic Product Listings:** Discover an ever-updating selection of near-expiry products from local retailers, clearly displaying original and discounted prices.  
- **🧠 Intelligent Dynamic Pricing:** Our AI-powered engine evaluates factors like expiry dates, demand, and user interest to calculate optimal, real-time pricing.  
- **⚡ Real-Time Updates:** Powered by Firebase Realtime Database, ensuring you never miss a fresh deal or restock alert.  
- **🎯 Personalized Recommendations:** Enjoy a tailored shopping experience with smart suggestions based on your preferences and shopping habits.  
- **🌱 Eco-Score Dashboard:** Visualize your personal impact—track how much waste you’ve helped prevent and see your contribution to a greener planet.  
- **🤝 Community Engagement:** Join a thriving community of waste warriors. Share recipes, hacks, and stories to inspire others.  
- **🌐 Multilingual Support:** Built to be inclusive and accessible with support for multiple languages for a global user base.

### 📲 Installation Guide

#### 💻 Using Android Studio (Emulator or Physical Device)

1. **Open the Project:**
   - Open Android Studio.
   - Select **“Open an existing project.”**
   - Navigate to the cloned folder and open the `build.gradle` file.

2. **Build & Run:**
   - Connect your Android device (with USB debugging enabled) or launch an emulator.
   - Click the green **Run** button.
   - Choose your target device.
   - The app will compile and install automatically.

---

### 📦 Direct APK Installation (Physical Device)

1. **Locate the APK:**
   - Find the APK at:  
     `app/build/outputs/apk/release/` or  
     `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/`
   - Transfer the APK file to your Android device.

2. **Enable Unknown Sources:**
   - On your device:  
     `Settings > Apps & Notifications > Special App Access > Install Unknown Apps`
   - Select your file manager app and enable **“Allow from this source.”**

3. **Install the APK:**
   - Open your file manager.
   - Locate the APK file and tap it.
   - Follow the on-screen instructions to install.

### ⚙️ How It Works

- **Retailers:** Register on FoodLoops and upload near-expiry product listings.  
- **AI-Powered Pricing:** Our algorithm calculates discounts based on expiry dates, demand, and user activity.  
- **Live Updates:** Product availability and prices update in real time.  
- **Smart Shopping:** Consumers browse, get personalized recommendations, and check out securely.  
- **Impact Tracking:** Users monitor their Eco-Scores to track how their shopping reduces food waste.
